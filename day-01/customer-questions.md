# AI Tutor Customer Discovery Questions

1. Who will use the system?
2. What problem are we solving?
3. What data will it access?
4. Does student information enter the model?
5. How many users?
6. What authentication system already exists?
7. Where should the application run?
8. What happens if the AI gives a bad answer?
9. Who reviews questionable responses?
10. What does success look like?
11. Would I need to give some responsible AI training to staff before going to production?
12. How much AI knowledge does every school in the district have generally? 
13. What kind of approval would we need to roll this out to schools?
